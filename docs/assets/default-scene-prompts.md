# StageMark default scenes — image prompt pack

> Historical generation brief. The 9 September selection supersedes the original three-scene plan: only Office & professional and Care & service from this pack are bundled, plus six separately generated Australian sectors. Operations & field is excluded. Existing PNGs were imported unchanged, including the selected reception artwork. See `bundled-scenes.md` for the implemented collection; these prompts are provenance, not regeneration instructions.

Generate three excellent, reusable workplace photographs. These are the highest-value image-generation assets for the first default collection: believable environments composed around a live phone demo. Phone geometry, logo placement, shadows and export belong in the app, where they stay crisp and adjustable.

## The first collection

| App name | Environment | Useful demo contexts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Care & service | Welcoming reception and service workplace | Care, community services, hospitality, customer-facing teams |
| Operations & field | Dispatch area opening onto a light industrial workplace | Logistics, manufacturing, maintenance, utilities, deskless teams |
| Office & professional | Everyday team workspace | Corporate services, professional services, employee workflows |

These are broad settings rather than claims to represent every industry. Three distinctive environments are enough for the first release. People, seasonal variants and alternate colourways can wait until real demos show a need.

## How to generate

Copy each complete prompt below into ChatGPT, one at a time. Request one image per prompt and download the original file, rather than taking a screenshot. Each prompt is self-contained. For a more consistent collection, attach the first successful image when generating the next one and say: “Match its photographic treatment and exposure, while following the new environment and composition.”

Ask for wide 16:9 landscape at the highest available native resolution. PNG is preferred; a high-quality JPEG is also usable. If the generator returns another aspect ratio, keep the original: we will assess cropping during packaging. Renaming downloads is optional; the suggested filenames simply identify the three images. Do not generate a contact sheet.

## 1. Care & service

Suggested filename: **stagemark-care-service.png**

~~~text
Create one premium, photorealistic workplace photograph to be used as a reusable desktop backdrop behind a live mobile app demonstration.

SETTING
A fictional, unbranded reception in a modern community care and service workplace. Warm, capable and welcoming, with the practical quality of an excellent real facility. On the left, a modest reception counter in pale oak and warm off-white, with a lowered accessible section. On the right, two comfortable commercial lounge chairs, a small side table and one restrained indoor plant. Beyond reception, a softly visible glazed corridor gives the scene believable depth. The setting should also feel plausible for community services or a customer service centre. There are no people present.

COMPOSITION
Wide landscape, 16:9. Viewpoint of a standing employee looking naturally across reception, approximately 1.5 metres above the floor, using a 35 mm full-frame photographic perspective. Keep vertical architecture straight. The central third must be visually quiet: a softly lit, warm neutral wall and open circulation space, with no important object or strong vertical edge directly behind the centre. A tall phone will be added later in this area and must feel like the foreground subject. Put useful environmental detail in the outer thirds, without cramming it against the edges. The scene must still read clearly if the phone is moved left or right.

Leave the upper-right corner as a naturally quiet, evenly lit stretch of pale wall, and the lower-left corner as a subdued, uncluttered counter frontage. Each should allow a small logo to be overlaid later without covering an important object. These are natural parts of the photograph, not drawn panels or empty labels. Allow generous cropping room around all edges.

PHOTOGRAPHIC TREATMENT
Soft morning daylight from the left, neutral-warm white balance, gently lifted shadows, controlled window highlights and rich but restrained natural colour. Tactile wood, upholstery and glass with physically convincing detail. The environment should be recognisable, with only subtle background softness. Aim for a carefully photographed real workplace: lived-in enough to be credible, clean enough for a presentation. Avoid the appearance of a luxury hotel, staged showroom or glossy architectural CGI.

OUTPUT
One full-bleed photograph at the highest available native resolution. No branding, logos, letters, numbers, signage, artwork containing text, watermarks, people, hands, phones, tablets, prominent computer screens, interface graphics, borders or presentation slides. No vignette, extreme wide-angle distortion, exaggerated blur or artificial lens flare.
~~~

## 3. Office & professional


## One repair prompt, when needed

Use this with an otherwise strong generated image if it competes with the phone. Repair only the issue that is actually present.

~~~text
Edit this image while preserving its workplace, photographic realism, camera position, lighting and material palette. Simplify the central third into a naturally quiet area suitable behind a tall foreground phone that will be added separately. Remove distracting objects, bright highlights and strong vertical edges from that area, preserving convincing architecture and depth. Keep useful workplace cues in the outer thirds. Preserve naturally quiet upper-right and lower-left corners for optional logo overlays. Remove any accidental text or branding. Do not add a phone, logo, artificial blur patch, blank graphic panel or other interface element. Return one full-resolution photograph.
~~~

## Packaging and the small branding enhancement

Current StageMark supports a backdrop, crop/zoom, a movable native phone frame and PNG/desktop output. It does **not yet have a separate logo layer**. These photographs work with the current app immediately; branding is a proposed small follow-up when the downloaded assets are packaged.

The intended first version is:

- Three clearly named default scenes, with the phone centred initially. Original default assets stay intact when a user customises a scene.
- One optional customer-provided transparent logo per saved scene. Use the actual logo file; no generative reconstruction is needed.
- Four corner positions, a size control and Remove. Start in the upper-right; use the lower-left if the composition calls for it. Keep the logo proportional and inset from the edges.
- A restrained light or dark backing plate when contrast requires it. Render the plate and padding in code, and include the same logo composition in preview, PNG export and desktop output.
- Keep the logo separate from the background and phone. Swapping a brand should not require regenerating the scene or disturbing its layout. Use screen-space corner placement, rather than pretending a logo is physically printed onto a perspective wall.

There is no need for new generated app icons, phone bezels, logo placeholders, patterns or decorative illustrations in this baseline. A photorealistic hand cutout could be useful later, but it would need an additional foreground layer, careful phone alignment and anatomy checks. It is deliberately outside this three-image batch.

## Acceptance before these become defaults

Judge each image at actual desktop size with StageMark's phone visible. Keep an image only if the workplace is immediately recognisable, the phone is the focal point, the central and corner areas remain usable, and there is no fabricated text, branding or implausible architecture. Check centre, left and right phone positions, then 16:9 and 16:10 crops. Check a dark and a light sample logo during implementation. If one image fails, repair or replace that image rather than expanding the collection.

At packaging time, inspect the original downloads, keep the best three, make app-sized derivatives without stretching, strip unnecessary metadata and record that the defaults were AI-generated. Retain originals outside the shipped bundle. No supplied customer photograph or customer logo becomes a bundled public default without a separate decision.
